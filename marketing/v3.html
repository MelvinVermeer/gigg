The gig page has it all

Date and times, the venue with a map link, the promoter's number, notes like the dress code, and files like the contract and rider. Booking and payment status sit right there too.

  • Lead → option → confirmed
  • Open → invoiced → paid
  • Google & Apple Calendar feed

Availability with a reason

Ask everyone with one tap. They answer yes, maybe or no from the email, no account needed, and a maybe can say why. The gig shows the count at a glance: 11

  • No login to answer
  • Push notifications
  • Re-ask if the date moves

Everybody does

Build it from sets, songs and reusable blocks. Running times add up as you go, and every change saves at once. On the night, show mode puts it on screen in big type, and it works offline.

  • Show mode
  • Lyrics mode
  • Print one A4 per set
  • Offline

Look it up, typos and all

Every song keeps its key, BPM, tuning, notes, lyrics and attached charts or demos. Search finds it even when you can't spell it.

  • Custom song fields
  • Files up to 100 MB
  • Markdown notes

And the boring stuff, done right

Nothing deleted

Items are archived, never removed, and every change shows who made it and what it was before.

Members edit. Readers, like the sound engineer, only look.
