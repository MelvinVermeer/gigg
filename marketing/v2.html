Set 1 · The booking3 songs · 11:02

Every gig in one list

Lead to confirmed 4:12

Track each booking from first enquiry to a confirmed date, and payment from open to invoiced to paid. A confirmed gig in the past counts as played.

Venues & contacts 3:20

Type a new venue or promoter straight into the event form. Gigg remembers them, with a map link to the address.

In your calendar 3:30

Subscribe from Google or Apple Calendar and your gigs show up next to everything else. Contracts and riders go on the event as files.

Set 2 · The band2 songs · 7:45

Who can make it?

Pick who's playing and ask. Everyone gets an email with yes, maybe and no buttons. They answer without logging in, and a maybe can say why.

  • Answers show up on the event as a small green, amber and red bar.
  • Change the date, and Gigg shows who needs asking again.
  • Push notifications when you're asked or added.
Set 3 · The songs3 songs · 12:08

Your whole repertoire, searchable

Key, BPM, tuning 3:32

Plus notes, lyrics, tags and any fields your band adds, like "Lead vocal". You choose which ones show on the setlist.

Charts & demos 4:26

Attach PDFs and recordings to a song, up to 100 MB each, and play them right from the song page.

Forgives typos 4:10

Type "lighthose" and you still find Lighthouse Keeper. Search covers songs, gigs, venues and contacts.

Set 4 · Showtime2 songs · 8:15

Big type, no signal needed

Show mode fills the screen with the set in huge letters. Swipe for the next set and the screen stays on. Lyrics mode does the same, one song at a time.

  • Upcoming gigs and their setlists are saved to your phone automatically.
  • Print gives one A4 per set, black on white.
  • Install it on your home screen like any app.
